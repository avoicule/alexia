Lasagna alla Ferrarese
6 dried pasta sheets, 300gr beef mince, 50gr Parma ham, 1 soft Italian sausage, 45ml olive oil, 2 carrots, 1 union, 2 stalks of celery, 200gr Parmigiano Reggiano, 2 cans of peeled tomatoes (800 grams), 500ml of bechamel, 200ml of wine
Preheat the oven on 180C. Dice the veggies into a small dice (brunoise) and fry them lightly until they soften. Fry off the meats on high temperature (cut the ham and take the sausage out of the casing). Deglaze the pan with the wine and add salt and pepper. Crush the tomatoes finely and add them to the mix, simmer for 30 minutes on low heat. Prepare the bechamel sauce. Mix half of the sauce with the meat mixture. Then build the lasagna in an oven dish. Start with pasta sheets on the bottom, and then add the meat mixture, then some bechamel sauce and grate some of the Parmigiano on top. Repeat this process until you run out (2-3 layers of pasta). Bake for 30 minutes in the oven.
